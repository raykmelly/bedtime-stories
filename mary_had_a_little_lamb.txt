Mary and Her Little Lamb

Once upon a time a girl named Mary lived on a small farm at the bottom of a green hill.
Mary loved all the animals on the farm, but one little lamb was her very favorite.
He had soft, creamy wool and a tiny black nose, and he was born on the coldest night of the spring.

Mary took care of him from the very beginning.
She fed him warm milk from a bottle, made him a bed of straw by the stove, and sang to him when he was sleepy.

As the lamb grew, he followed Mary everywhere she went.
He trotted behind her to the garden.
He trotted behind her to the well.
He even trotted behind her down the dusty road, with his little hooves going clip-clop, clip-clop.

One Monday morning, Mary picked up her schoolbag and headed out the door.
She did not notice the small, woolly shadow tiptoeing along behind her.
