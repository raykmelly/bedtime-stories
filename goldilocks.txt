Goldilocks and the Three Bears

Once upon a time a girl named Goldilocks lived in a house at the edge of the woods.
In those days curls of hair were called "locks."
She was "Goldilocks" because golden hair ran down her head and shoulders.
Goldilocks loved to explore.
Every morning she skipped down the path behind her house, counting the birds and chasing the butterflies.
Her mother always warned her, "Don't wander too far into the woods, Goldilocks."
But one sunny morning, a sweet smell drifted through the trees.
Goldilocks followed her nose, past the old oak, over the little stream, and deeper than she had ever gone before.
There, in a clearing, stood a cozy cottage with a bright blue door.
She knocked once. She knocked twice.
Nobody answered, and the door swung open all by itself.
