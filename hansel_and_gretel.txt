Hansel and Gretel

Once upon a time a poor woodcutter lived in a tiny cottage at the edge of a great, dark forest.
He lived there with his two children, a boy named Hansel and a girl named Gretel.
Hansel was brave and clever.
Gretel was kind and quick, and the two of them were best friends.
That winter was the hardest anyone could remember.
The snow piled high, the woodcutter could not sell his firewood, and there was hardly any bread left in the house.
Each night the family went to bed with their tummies growling.
One evening, Hansel and Gretel lay awake and heard their parents whispering about a plan to take the children deep into the forest in the morning.
Gretel began to cry, but Hansel patted her hand.
"Don't worry," he said. "I have an idea."
When the house was quiet, Hansel tiptoed outside.
The moon was bright, and the little white pebbles in the yard shone like silver coins.
He filled his pockets with as many as they would hold.
